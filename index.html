What is Claude to RIPCO?

A SEEM tool. And your digital assistant.

Claude is a large language model from Anthropic. At RIPCO we use it two ways: as a SEEM tool (Search, Extract, Explain, Model) for research and analysis, and as a digital assistant that can operate our tools on your behalf.

The same Claude powers both. What changes is the interface — Chat is a research tab, Cowork is an agent with hands on your files and Monday.com. The Skills Library below is Claude's playbook: skills tell it exactly how to route a request to the right board, the right column, and the right subitem.

The short version: Chat answers questions. Cowork does the work — including uploading files directly into Monday.com.

Chat

Read-only

The classic Claude conversation. Great for questions, drafting, thinking through problems, and one-off research.

  • Ask questions, get answers
  • Draft emails, summaries, memos
  • Web search for market intel
  • Cannot upload files to Monday.com
  • Cannot read your local computer
  • No skills, no plugins

Cowork

Full agent

Claude with hands. Point it at a folder and it works alongside you — reading files, running scripts, calling Monday, generating branded docs.

  • Everything Chat does, plus:
  • Uploads files directly into Monday.com boards
  • Reads leases, spreadsheets, PDFs from your desktop
  • Generates DOCX / HTML / PDF deliverables
  • Runs the full RIPCO plugin & skill library
  • Talks to Monday and any connector you've authorized
↑
Cowork uploads to Monday.com. Drop a Property Shark export into Cowork and it lands as enriched sales-comp items — no copy-paste, no manual column mapping.
Claude & Monday.com

Monday is where the work lives. Claude is how it gets done.

Every RIPCO deal, listing, lead, tenant, and marketing project lives on a Monday.com board. Claude — via Cowork and our plugins — is the operator that reads and writes those boards without you leaving the conversation.

Claude reads Monday first

Before writing anything, our skills fetch the exact board schema, column IDs, and existing linked records. This is why the agent never guesses a column name or duplicates a property.

Claude writes to Monday with rules

Each skill enforces RIPCO's write conventions — parent items vs subitems, signage and e-blast dual-writes, contact-relation preservation, and confirm-before-write on every submission.

Claude generates from Monday

Status reports, tour surveys, BOV decks, and availability snapshots pull live data from Monday. Update the board, regenerate the deliverable — no more stale exports.

Start here · Required setup

Connect Monday.com to Claude.

Nothing in this library works until Claude can reach your Monday account. This is a one-time, two-minute setup you do yourself — and it's the single most important step.

  1. Open the connector directory

    In the Claude desktop app or on claude.ai, go to Settings and find the Connectors page. This is where every external system Claude can reach gets authorized.

    Settings › Connectors › Browse connectors

  2. Find monday.com and click Connect

    Search the directory for monday. You're looking for the official monday.com connector — it appears with the monday logo. Click Connect.

    If you don't see it in the directory, ask Claude: "search the connector registry for monday" and it will surface the install option for you.

  3. Sign in with your RIPCO monday account

    A monday.com login window opens. Use your RIPCO email — the same account you use for the boards every day. Do not use a personal monday account; the boards won't be there.

    Monday will show you which permissions Claude is requesting. Review and approve.

  4. Confirm it's live

    Back in Claude, the monday.com connector should show as Connected. Open a Cowork session and ask it something simple that requires a board read — if it answers with your real data, you're done.

  5. Use Cowork, not Chat, for board work

    Chat can read some connector data, but only Cowork can write to Monday, upload files, and run the RIPCO plugins. If a skill isn't firing, check you're in Cowork first.

What Claude can and can't see

Claude inherits your monday permissions — nothing more. If you can't open a board, neither can Claude. Every write is confirmed with you before it happens, and nothing is written on your behalf without a yes.

If something isn't working

Skills aren't firing
You're probably in Chat. Switch to Cowork — plugins and board writes only run there.
"I can't find that board"
Either you signed in with a personal monday account, or you don't have access to that board. Check the account email in Settings › Connectors.
Connector shows Disconnected
Tokens expire. Click Reconnect and sign in again — your data is untouched.
Still stuck
Bring it to office hours. Nine times out of ten it's the wrong account or the wrong mode.
